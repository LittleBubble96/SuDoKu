Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production game app icon / Chinese mini-program avatar, one square image, designed to read very clearly at 144 x 144 pixels and also under a circular avatar mask.
Primary request: design an original, polished icon for our clean, modern number-placement puzzle game. The project's current UI is white with vivid blue #2086FF accents, very pale blue #EEF6FF panels, dark navy #0A1944 numbers, and rounded geometry. Its homepage already uses a simple 3 by 3 board with the digits 1, 5, 9 on a diagonal. Evolve that existing visual identity into a stronger standalone app icon.
Composition: flat front-facing, perfectly centered single white rounded-square puzzle board occupying approximately 66 percent of the total square canvas. Precisely two vertical and two horizontal blue dividing lines form exactly 3 rows and 3 columns. Generous square cells, balanced rounded outer corners, clean even line weights, all lines align accurately. Digits: a single large navy "1" in the upper-left cell; a single large vivid blue "5" in the center cell; a single large navy "9" in the lower-right cell. All six other cells are empty. Center cell is filled pale blue #B3DEFD as the selected-cell state, integrated flush within the board. Digits are bold, readable geometric sans serif, consistently sized and optically centered. These are the only text.
Background: full bleed vivid sky-blue square, close to #2086FF, with a very restrained lighter-blue upper-left gradient. Do not pre-round the outer canvas. Keep the board and all important content well within the central circular safe area, avoiding clipping in a round avatar. A delicate soft shadow under the board adds just a little separation.
Style: elegant, friendly casual puzzle branding; crisp vector-like raster finish; simple solid forms; excellent small-size contrast; restrained shallow depth, mostly flat. Match the project's bright clean blue-and-white visual language. Final finished icon only, not a mockup or a sheet.
Avoid: pencils, erasers, hands, mascots, extra objects, stars, badges, ribbons, decorative lettering, Chinese characters, app title, watermark, full 9 by 9 board, tiny numbers, repeated digits, tilted board, perspective, heavy outlines around each tile, thick toy-like 3D, glossy plastic, neon glow, black background, border around canvas, presentation labels.
